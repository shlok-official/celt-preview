What is Celt Industries?

Celt Industries is a TSBC-licensed refrigeration and gas contractor based in Langley, BC. We install, maintain and repair industrial and commercial refrigeration, HVAC/R, mechanical and electrical systems. We also install gas detection and ozone filtration systems for food processors, property managers, marine and fishing operators, manufacturers and commercial facilities across the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland.

Services

What we install, service and repair

One licensed team for the cold side, the air side and the safety systems that protect them.

Industrial and commercial refrigeration

Cold rooms, freezers, walk-in coolers, racks and process cooling. Installation, repair and upgrades.

Marine and fishing refrigeration

Reliable cooling for vessels and dockside operations, built for wet, salty working conditions.

HVAC/R

Heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration for commercial and industrial buildings.

Mechanical service

Preventative maintenance, repairs and upgrades for the mechanical equipment your site depends on.

Electrical

Troubleshooting and maintenance for controls, motors and power to your refrigeration and HVAC equipment.

Gas detection and monitoring

Fixed gas detection and monitoring systems that alert your team to a leak before it becomes an incident.

Ozone filtration

Planning and installation of ozone filtration to help control odour and air quality.

Energy-saving upgrades

Higher-efficiency refrigeration retrofits that cut energy use without cutting performance.

Preventative maintenance plans. A scheduled plan of inspections and adjustments catches wear early, so a failure does not turn into spoiled product or a shutdown. Ask us to set one up for your site.

About

Hands-on trade expertise, led by the founder

Celt Industries was founded in 2022 by Clayton Betker, a journeyman refrigeration mechanic and electrician who leads as service manager. Having a refrigeration mechanic and an electrician in one person means faster diagnosis when a fault sits between the mechanical and electrical sides of a system.

We are a locally based team serving the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland, and we hold TSBC refrigeration and gas contractor licenses.
