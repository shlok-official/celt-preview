About Celt Industries

A locally based industrial services company serving the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland.

Our story

Hands-on trade expertise, led by the founder

Celt Industries was founded in 2022 by Clayton Betker, a journeyman refrigeration mechanic and electrician who leads as service manager.

Having a refrigeration mechanic and an electrician in one person means faster diagnosis when a fault sits between the mechanical and electrical sides of a system. Customers deal with one team, not a chain of trades.

We serve food processors, property managers, marine and fishing operators, manufacturers and commercial facilities across the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland.
