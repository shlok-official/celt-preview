FAQ

Ozone filtration questions

Is ozone safe?

Ozone can irritate lungs at high levels. A good system is sized and controlled so people are not exposed. We plan every system for safe use.

Where is ozone filtration used?

It is used where odours are a problem, such as kitchens, cold storage, waste areas and processing spaces.

Do you handle planning and installation?

Yes. We plan the system, install it and tie it into your ventilation.
