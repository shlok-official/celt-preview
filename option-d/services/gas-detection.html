Overview

Gas detection and monitoring systems in the Fraser Valley

Gas leaks are hard to see and can be dangerous. A good detection system warns people early and can trigger ventilation or alarms.

We install and service gas detection and monitoring systems, and we keep them tested so they work when you need them.

When to call

Signs you need help

Catching problems early costs less than an emergency repair.

  • Detectors are past their calibration date
  • Frequent false alarms
  • Detectors have never been tested
  • New equipment was added without detection
  • Staff are unsure what the alarms mean
  • An audit or insurer has asked for records

FAQ

Gas detection questions

Why do I need gas detection?

Gas detection warns people early about leaks. That helps protect staff and product, and it supports refrigeration safety requirements.

How often should detectors be tested?

Follow the maker's schedule and your site rules. Many sites test and calibrate at least once a year. We can set up a schedule for you.

Do you install and service detection systems?

Yes. We install gas detection and monitoring systems and keep them tested.
