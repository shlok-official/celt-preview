When to call

Signs you need help

Catching problems early costs less than an emergency repair.

  • Breakers or overloads trip repeatedly
  • Motors hum, overheat or will not start
  • Controllers show errors or lose settings
  • Sensors give wrong readings
  • Burn marks or smells at panels
  • Equipment restarts by itself

FAQ

Electrical questions

What electrical work do you do?

We troubleshoot and maintain the electrical side of refrigeration, HVAC and mechanical equipment, including controls, wiring, starters and sensors.

Who does the electrical work?

Our founder and service manager, Clayton Betker, is a journeyman refrigeration mechanic and electrician.

Can you fix a fault that could be mechanical or electrical?

Yes. Having both skills in one team means we find the cause without handing you between trades.
