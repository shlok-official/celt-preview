Industrial and commercial refrigeration in the Fraser Valley

When to call

Signs you need help

Catching problems early costs less than an emergency repair.

  • Temperatures drift or will not hold
  • Ice builds up on coils or around doors
  • The compressor starts and stops often
  • Unusual noise, vibration or smells
  • Energy bills rise with no change in use
  • Alarms or controller error codes appear

FAQ

Refrigeration questions

What types of refrigeration systems do you work on?

We work on industrial and commercial refrigeration, including cold rooms, freezers, walk-in coolers, racks and process cooling.

What are signs my cooler or freezer needs service?

Common signs are temperatures that drift, ice build-up, frequent compressor cycling, strange noises and rising energy bills. Call early. Small faults cost less to fix.

Do you offer emergency refrigeration repair?

Yes. We offer 24/7 emergency response in the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland.

Can you help me lower refrigeration energy costs?

Often, yes. Controls, defrost settings, fan motors and condenser condition all affect energy use. See our energy-saving upgrades.
