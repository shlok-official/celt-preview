Who we are

What is Celt Industries?

Celt Industries is a TSBC-licensed refrigeration and gas contractor based in Langley, BC. We install, maintain and repair industrial and commercial refrigeration, HVAC/R, mechanical and electrical systems. We also install gas detection and ozone filtration systems for food processors, property managers, marine and fishing operators, manufacturers and commercial facilities across the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland.

FAQ

Common questions

What does Celt Industries do?

Celt Industries is a British Columbia contractor that installs, services and repairs refrigeration, HVAC/R, mechanical and electrical systems, and installs gas detection and ozone filtration systems for industrial and commercial facilities.

Where does Celt Industries work?

Celt Industries serves the Fraser Valley and the Lower Mainland of British Columbia.

Does Celt Industries offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes. Celt Industries offers 24/7 emergency response for refrigeration and related system failures.

Is Celt Industries licensed?

Yes. Celt Industries holds TSBC refrigeration and gas contractor licenses.

Which industries does Celt Industries serve?

Food processing, property management, marine and fishing, industrial and manufacturing sites, and commercial facilities such as storefronts and businesses.

What is a preventative maintenance plan?

It is a scheduled set of inspections, cleaning and adjustments that catches wear and leaks early. It lowers the chance of an emergency breakdown, protects stored product and helps equipment run efficiently.

How do I book a consultation?
